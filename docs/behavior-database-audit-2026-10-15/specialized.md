Comparison inputs: target [`kevinbudz/realm-server`](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/tree/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6) at `bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6`; candidate reference [`ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom`](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/tree/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb) at `6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb` (2015-11-09). Differences below are evidence for review, not proof that the target is wrong. Scope is enemy behavior database definitions.

## Candyland

Both databases define Candy Gnome, Desire Troll, Gigacorn, Spoiled Creampuff, Big Creampuff, and Small Creampuff. The target also defines a larger custom roster in `Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs`—including MegaRototo, Cupcake, Candyland spawner markers, Gumball Machine, Fairy, Rototo/Tiny Rototo, Butterfly, Wishing Troll, Unicorn, Spilled IceCream, Beefy Fairy, and Hard Candy—which has no matching definitions in the candidate's `BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs`. That file has only the six shared names. The target XML catalog confirms several shared IDs: Candy Gnome, Spoiled Creampuff, Big Creampuff, and Small Creampuff appear in `[EmbeddedData_CandyLandCXML.xml:4](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Resources/GameData/EmbeddedData_CandyLandCXML.xml#L4),82,553,597`.

- Candy Gnome differs in progression and rewards. The target waits for a nearby player, pursues, then flees through distance-selected states; it drops the Candyland portal at 50% with a 30-second timeout and has a 0.01 threshold with Rock Candy and 15% gumballs ([Candyland.cs:16-55](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L16-L55)). Candidate definition is a simple stay-back/wander behavior, drops the portal at 50% with a 120-second timeout, and uses a 0.18 threshold with 50% gumballs ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:15-29](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L15-L29)).
- Desire Troll is materially reworked: target has a 60%-HP transition into a second attack phase, timed run/charge choices, and a close-range radial burst ([Candyland.cs:139-179](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L139-L179)); candidate has one continuous wander/grenade/shoot state ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:31-38](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L31-L38)). The target loot table is tier/gumball/Ring Pop based ([Candyland.cs:185-205](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L185-L205)), while candidate adds Fairy Plate, Pixie-Enchanted Sword, Seal of the Enchanted Forest, and Ring of Pure Wishes among its loot ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:39-63](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L39-L63)).
- Gigacorn has a timed alternating shot/pause cycle and a charge/stay-back priority in the target ([Candyland.cs:246-284](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L246-L284)); candidate uses wander, charge, and repeated projectile schedules in one state ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:65-100](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L65-L100)). Treat this as an encounter redesign or version difference; the names alone do not establish a regression.
- Spoiled Creampuff has a particularly clear spawn difference: target starts with zero Big Creampuffs and reproduces them on a 25-second cooldown, alongside its movement and attacks ([Candyland.cs:105-116](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L105-L116)); candidate configures `maxChildren: 2, initialSpawn: 2` and a 5-second spawn cooldown ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:101-106](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L101-L106)). Both Spawn primitives compute actual initial count as `(int)(maxChildren * initialSpawn)` ([Spawn.cs:14-19](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Behaviors/Spawn.cs#L14-L19); [Spawn.cs:18-22](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/behaviors/Spawn.cs#L18-L22)), so candidate computes four initial Big Creampuffs despite the cap of two. Its counter starts at four and increments on each successful initial spawn, leaving later Spawn emissions disabled; the cap is not enforced during initial entry. Target Big Creampuff uses `SpawnOnce` on entry to choose a random one, two, or three Small Creampuffs and has charge/stay-back behavior ([Candyland.cs:341-352](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L341-L352); [SpawnOnce.cs:6-31](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Behaviors/SpawnOnce.cs#L6-L31)). Candidate Big Creampuff computes one initial Small Creampuff from `maxChildren: 2, initialSpawn: 0.5`; its counter is then at the cap, so the 5-second cooldown does not emit another one, even after the initial child dies ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:131-137](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L131-L137); Spawn primitive lines above).
- Target Small Creampuff protects its Big Creampuff, charges, and stays near spawn ([Candyland.cs:353-361](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Candyland.cs#L353-L361)); candidate only wanders and shoots ([BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs:138-143](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CandyLand.cs#L138-L143)).

## Crystal

The target and candidate both define Mysterious Crystal, Crystal Prisoner, its clones, and its steeds. The target's Crystal encounter is largely structurally aligned, but two observable differences merit a check: candidate Mysterious Crystal has a 100% Deadwater Docks portal drop on death ([BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs:14-22](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs#L14-L22)), which is absent from target's definition ([Crystal.cs:16-24](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Crystal.cs#L16-L24)); and candidate Crystal Prisoner can spawn five steeds, while target caps that spawn at three ([BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs:77-80](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs#L77-L80); [Crystal.cs:79-83](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Crystal.cs#L79-L83)). Target activates the crystal encounter only after a player enters 10 units ([Crystal.cs:16-19](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Crystal.cs#L16-L19)), a gate not present in the candidate's idle state. Loot is also changed: candidate grants guaranteed Potion of Vitality and has 0.005/0.006 Crystal Wand/Sword chances ([BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs:229-238](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Crystal.cs#L229-L238)); target omits the guaranteed Vitality drop but raises those item chances to 0.05/0.06 ([Crystal.cs:229-235](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Crystal.cs#L229-L235)). These may reflect separate tuning revisions, but the item table delta is worth verifying against the intended 7.0.0 data.

## Shatters

There is no target `Game/Logic/Database/Shatters.cs`, and the target embedded XML object catalog has no Shatters IDs. The candidate `db/data/dat1.xml` does contain Shatters IDs, including Stone Paladin and Stone Knight (`:13239`, `:13212`); its `wServer.csproj` explicitly compiles the Shatters behavior database (`:221`). The behavior file defines Stone Paladin and Stone Knight ([BehaviorDb.Shatters.cs:17](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Shatters.cs#L17), `:72`), Ice Mage/Adept and generators (`:85-118`), Ice/Fire portals (`:119`, `:164`), Bridge Sentinel (`:209`), Twilight Archmage and Forgotten King (`:495`, `:593`), encounter chest/spawner (`:628-649`), and Inferno/Blizzard (`:684-695`). This establishes candidate-only coverage in these checked-in 2015 sources and data; the historical status in 2014 remains unverified.

## Other later/custom candidate groups

No same-named target database class exists for the following candidate groups. For the checked names below, the target embedded XML object catalog also has no corresponding object IDs, while candidate `db/data/dat1.xml` does. The candidate project explicitly compiles Belladonna (`wServer.csproj:219`), Draconis (`:156`), and EpicForestMaze (`:158`). Their absence is only a candidate-corpus finding; the 2015 repository cannot establish availability in the 2014 target version.

- Valentine's/Belladonna: `BehaviorDb.Belladonna.cs` defines Botany Bella, Bella Buds, Red/White/Yellow buds, and a loot balloon (`[BehaviorDb.Belladonna.cs:16-20](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Belladonna.cs#L16-L20),197,243,272,301,330`). The boss transforms on death into the loot balloon and copies damage (`:17-20`); the buds depend on boss existence and transform cyclically (`:197-200,243-310`). These checked IDs are absent from target `Resources/GameData/*.xml`; candidate data has Botany Bella/Bella Buds at `[dat1.xml:51363](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/db/data/dat1.xml#L51363),51416`.
- Lair of Draconis: the candidate's `BehaviorDb.Draconis.cs` defines only `NM Black Dragon God`, with a single projectile behavior (`:12-17`). Target XML has no such ID; candidate [dat1.xml:3196](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/db/data/dat1.xml#L3196) does. Because this candidate definition is only a placeholder, its absence is weak evidence of any practical coverage difference.
- Epic Forest Maze: candidate `BehaviorDb.EpicForestMaze.cs` defines Murderous Megamoth and larvae, an Epic Mama Megamoth transition, woodland goblins/squirrels, and three turret definitions (`:9-19,46-54,55-89,90-127,128-198`). The candidate's strongest encounter sequence is Epic Larva transforming on death into Epic Mama Megamoth (`:90-99`), whose 30%-HP transition makes it invulnerable before suicide (`:55-75`).
- Related candidate-only environment/custom definitions include Forest Maze (`BehaviorDb.ForestMaze.cs`, compiled via `wServer.csproj:159`), Forbidden Jungle (`BehaviorDb.ForbiddenJungle.cs`, compiled via `:228`), Drakes (`BehaviorDb.Drakes.cs`, compiled via `:157`), and generic event/specialized/tutorial groups (`BehaviorDb.Events.cs`, `:227`; `BehaviorDb.Specialized.cs`, `:244`; `BehaviorDb.Tutorial.cs`, `:246`).
