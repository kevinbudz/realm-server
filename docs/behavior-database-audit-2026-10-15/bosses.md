# Named event bosses and Oryx: behavior database comparison

Compared target `kevinbudz/realm-server` at `bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6` with reference `ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom` at `6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb` (2015-11-09). The reference is a later snapshot, not an authority for the 2014 7.0.0 behavior. The differences below are concrete drift and should not automatically be treated as defects. Links pin both sides to the compared commits.

## Oryx and castle

- **Oryx 1 (Oryx Chamber):** The target and reference share the `Oryx the Mad God 1` ID, 20% rage threshold, 20-second Slow phase, Ring Element dance, Guardian Element artifact phase, gaze, four-step Dance 2 loop, and transition into rage. The target declares the locked Wine Cellar portal and has Minion of Oryx `Spawn` plus `Reproduce` in both Slow and rage. The pinned Oryx definition carries the same portal and minion setup. The phase sequence is not a missing-phase case. See [target Oryx Chamber](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/OryxChamber.cs#L13) and [reference Oryx 1](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Oryx.cs#L107).
- **Oryx 2 (Wine Cellar):** The target adds `ScaleHP(amount: 30)` and actively tosses Monstrosity Scarab during rage. In the pinned file, the scarab toss is commented out; the rest of its main attack and prepare-rage/rage cycle is substantially the same. The target has a direct 100% Vitality Potion drop; the reference assigns the same guaranteed drop to its `MostDamagers(3)` group. This is a real rule difference in scaling, add pressure, and eligibility, but the later snapshot alone cannot establish which is historically correct. See [target Wine Cellar](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/WineCellar.cs#L13) and [reference Oryx 2](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Oryx.cs#L14).
- **Oryx Stone Guardians:** Both databases use the same guardian names, paired start, HP-based attack stages, and general projectile families. The reference Right Guardian adds map-position movement and a long “Circle” formation sequence (including 10 timed radial shots and a sideways barrage); the target instead has direct four-stage attack loops, with the Right Guardian orbiting its partner at the 50% stage. The reference script is tied to a different arena layout; the target source itself records its choice of the other map layout. Treat the phase mismatch as map/version divergence, not a demonstrated omitted attack. See [target guardians](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/OryxCastle.cs#L17) and [reference guardian choreography](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.OryxCastle.cs#L15).

## Realm event bosses

- **Pentaract:** This is a substantial redesign under the same names. The target makes `Pentaract` invincible until no `Pentaract Tower` remains, then suicides; each `Pentaract Tower Corpse` waits 15 seconds and transforms itself back into a tower. Target towers spawn up to five eyes, throw a 100-damage grenade every 5 seconds, transform into a corpse, and transfer damage to both `Pentaract` and its corpse. The reference puts a `PentaractStar(250)` and 15-second tower-respawn order loop on Pentaract, while tower spawn is initial-only, grenade cooldown is 2 seconds, and damage goes to the corpse at multiplier 2. Its eye uses a different swirl and fires every 200 ms; target eyes protect/orbit towers and fire every second. These differences change respawn ownership, attack cadence, target damage routing, and eye behavior. The target's extra damage transfer and inline crash comment are notable implementation concerns, but the reference comparison does not prove them wrong. See [target Pentaract](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Pentaract.cs#L16) and [reference Pentaract](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Pentaract.cs#L15).
- **Grand Sphinx:** The target has three timed attacks (6s, 6.2s, 6s) separated by 2s invulnerable transitions, reproduces Horrid Reapers up to 30 within radius 4, and orders them to die after falling below 15% HP. The reference instead uses Blind Attack, Armor Break Attack, and Weaken Attack in a roughly 10s-per-attack loop with 2s transitions; it directly spawns up to eight Reapers and orders them to leave at 15%. Loot also differs: target lists Vitality/Wisdom at 10% each and Juggernaut at 0.4%; reference awards the two potions to top three damage dealers and uses 0.5% Juggernaut. This is a complete behavior variant, not a small omitted subphase. See [target Sphinx](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Sphinx.cs#L16) and [reference Sphinx](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Sphinx.cs#L14).
- **Skull Shrine:** Both use the `Skull Shrine` ID, the ring shot, and red/blue flaming skull minions, but spawn pressure and loot differ. Target reproduces each skull type with density 20/radius 40 on a 500ms cooldown and lists standard high-tier gear, six stat potions at 10%, six cloths at 1%, and Orb of Conflict at 0.1%. Reference combines capped spawning (red 8/5s; blue 10/1s) with reproduction densities 8 and 10 in radius 10; it uses a different tier table and 0.5% Orb chance, and makes stat potions top-three-damage loot. The target's much wider/higher reproduction cap is a concrete tuning difference. See [target Skull Shrine](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/SkullShrine.cs#L16) and [reference Skull Shrine](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Events.cs#L16).
- **Hermit God:** Target alternates an invisible/tentacle wait, wakes by tossing eight minions, waits for a Whirlpool, then uses two 6-second attacks before looping. The reference begins with an invisible wave that tosses many minions and seven tentacles, checks for tentacle death, then runs Whirlpool attacks for 20s followed by a 17s rage phase before returning invisible. It also uses different minion shooting patterns. This is a major cycle and add-count redesign. See [target Hermit](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Hermit.cs#L16) and [reference Hermit](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Events.cs#L67).
- **Ghost King:** The `Ghost King` and `Actual Ghost King` behavior, player-count evaluation branches, timed ghost waves, 140-second timeout, and transformation are functionally aligned. No material missing phase was found in this pairing. See [target Ghost King](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/GhostKing.cs#L16) and [reference Ghost King](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.GhostKing.cs#L14).
- **Davy Jones / Ghost Ship:** Target Davy starts with a player-range gate and a 200ms invulnerable floating buffer, then becomes vulnerable for 2.5s; the reference starts directly in Floating, fires at half the target cooldown (1000/2000ms vs 2000/4000ms), and leaves the vulnerable window open for 5s. Target's lantern turns on after the lantern-off entity drops below 25% HP; reference transforms on death. Drop tables differ substantially: target has Davy-specific drops including Davy's Key and gems, while reference uses a broad tier table plus Wine Cellar Incantation. The Ghost Ship itself is also a different phase script: target's named `Phase1+2` begins after a 3s start and 300ms preattack, while reference begins with an idle/damage pause, then a 15s start phase. These affect timing and encounter gates; without an era-specific source, they remain observed differences rather than confirmed historical defects. See [target Davy](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/DavyJones.cs#L16), [reference Davy](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.DavyJones.cs#L14), [target Ghost Ship](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/GhostShip.cs#L16), and [reference Ghost Ship](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.GhostShip.cs#L154).
- **Phoenix Lord:** The attack, Phoenix Egg transformation, rebirth attacks, and Pyre adds align. The target's Phoenix Reborn has six 3% cloth drops; the reference instead gives a 0.2% Wine Cellar Incantation. This is a drop-table difference, not an absent rebirth phase. See [target Phoenix](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/Phoenix.cs#L16) and [reference Phoenix](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Phoenix.cs#L14).
- **Cube God (active reference definition resolved):** Both target and reference XML identify Cube God as object type `0x0d59`. The target has two predictive shot schedules and reproduces up to 10 Cube Overseers in radius 30; the Overseers reproduce Defender and Blaster minions. In the reference project, the compiled file is `BehaviorDb.Cube.cs`, whose string-ID initializer resolves `Cube God` through XML and adds it to the runtime definitions. `BehaviorDb.CubeGod.cs` is not included by `wServer.csproj`; it declares a static behavior field, while the loader enumerates only nonpublic instance fields. Therefore the legacy numeric-ID behavior is inactive in this build. The active `Cube.cs` definition uses a different single 9-shot schedule, then configures Spawn with maximum 5 and initial factors 3, 5, and 5 for Overseers, Defenders, and Blasters. Since Spawn computes `initialSpawn = (int)(maxChildren * factor)`, this attempts 15/25/25 initial minions, above each configured cap; its counter starts at those computed values and the reference initial-spawn loop does not increment it, so further Spawn emissions are disabled because each starting count already exceeds its cap. Its loot also uses Dirk of Cronus at 1% and Attack/Dexterity potions at 50%, versus target Dirk at 0.1% and each listed stat potion at 10%. This is a confirmed behavior and loot divergence for the shared XML ID, including an abnormal reference spawn configuration. See [target XML object](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Resources/GameData/EmbeddedData_EncountersCXML.xml#L333), [target behavior](https://github.com/kevinbudz/realm-server/blob/bb75bc1e438b465986a8ada354b6332f69e0e3a6/Game/Logic/Database/CubeGod.cs#L16), [reference XML object](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/db/data/dat1.xml#L23624), [reference compiled Cube.cs inclusion](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/wServer.csproj#L222), [reference active behavior](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.Cube.cs#L14), [excluded legacy field](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/db/BehaviorDb.CubeGod.cs#L16), and [loader reflection flags](https://github.com/ossimc82/fabiano-swagger-of-doom/blob/6fd20aad4a7905b13f25389c68368a942a2b68cb/wServer/logic/BehaviorDb.cs#L56).

## Scope notes

- **Reviewed IDs:** Oryx the Mad God 1 and 2; Oryx Stone Guardian Right and Left; Cube God, Cube Overseer, Cube Defender, and Cube Blaster; Grand Sphinx and Horrid Reaper; Phoenix Lord, Phoenix Egg, Phoenix Reborn, Birdman Chief, and Birdman; Pentaract, Pentaract Eye, Pentaract Tower, and Pentaract Tower Corpse; Skull Shrine, Red Flaming Skull, and Blue Flaming Skull; Ghost King, Actual Ghost King, Ghost Master, Small Ghost, Medium Ghost, Large Ghost; Ghost Ship and Davy Jones (including their principal named adds and lanterns); Hermit God and its tentacle/minion/Whirlpool support definitions; and Ent Ancient. The file-level comparisons for Oryx Castle, Oryx Chamber, Wine Cellar, Phoenix, Pentaract, Sphinx, Skull Shrine, Ghost King, Ghost Ship/Davy Jones, and Hermit God are detailed above. Ent Ancient was only spot-checked at the high-level definition; no full phase/loot audit is claimed for it.
- **Avatar applicability:** No `Avatar` behavior definition exists under the target `Game/Logic/Database`. The reference defines `shtrs Defense System` as Avatar, but applicability to the target's 2014 scope is unverified here. Defer classification to the target XML ID/type inventory rather than treating the omission as a confirmed gap.
- **Coverage limits:** This is a comparison of the named definitions above, not an exhaustive realm-event or missing-enemy inventory. It does not audit ordinary gods or dungeon bosses outside the Oryx behaviors named above. Differences called out as material are the behavior, timing, add-pressure, or loot mismatches described above; the Oryx 1 phase chain and Ghost King progression align, and Phoenix's rebirth chain aligns apart from drops. The later reference snapshot cannot determine which divergent variant is historically correct for 2014.
